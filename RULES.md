# STACKED Rules

## Laws

### The deal

- **Law 1.** Hand size 4. Each round opens with 4 cards to each player and 4 to the board. When all hands are empty, the next hand deals 4 to each player and none to the board. Four hands per round.

### Turns

- **Law 2.** CAPTURE continues the turn for as long as the capturer still holds cards; PLACE ends it.
- **Law 3.** On your turn you must capture or place; there is no pass.
- **Law 4.** Any card in the hand may be placed; nothing restricts which card, and nothing forces a capture when one is available.
- **Law 5.** A player holding no cards is skipped until the next deal.
- **Law 6.** When every other player is out of cards, the last player's turn works as normal: capture as many times as they can, or place. Once they place a card, the game places the rest of their hand on the board, and the hand ends.

### Capturing

- **Law 7.** A capture is one base card, from the hand or the board, and one to three filled combo slots. A match is a card of the base's rank; a sum is a group of cards whose values add up to the base's value.
- **Law 8.** One combo per slot, unlimited matches per slot, and never a match and a sum in one slot.
- **Law 9.** Per slot: every filled combo slot needs at least one card from the opposite source to the base (base from hand, every slot needs a board card; base from board, every slot needs a hand card).
- **Law 10.** Face cards (K/Q/J) make pairs only, never sums. The 10 is not a face card - it sums.
- **Law 11.** Aces count as 1 in sums. Cards 2 to 10 count as their number.
- **Law 12.** A card may be used only once in a capture.
- **Law 13.** Every card in a capture scores at its value in the scoring table, base included.

### Round end

- **Law 14.** A round ends when every hand and the deck are empty.
- **Law 15.** Take the Table - at round end, the last player to capture in that round takes every card left on the board, scored at their values in the scoring table. If nobody captured that round, nobody takes the table. Taking the table is not a capture.
- **Law 16.** At round end, Take the Table is scored before the game-end check.

### Game end

- **Law 17.** A game ends at the end of a round when at least one player is at or above the target score and the top score belongs to one player alone; that player wins. If the top score is tied, an overtime round is played.

## Scoring table

| Card | Points |
|---|---|
| A | 15 |
| K, Q, J, 10 | 10 |
| 2 to 9 | 5 |

## Classic

- **Classic rule 1.** Always three players, the human and two bots.
- **Classic rule 2.** One standard 52-card deck, shuffled fresh at the start of every round. Nothing carries to the next round except the scores.
- **Classic rule 3.** The first dealer is chosen at random. The player after the dealer goes first and leads every hand of that round. When a round ends, the deal passes to the next seat.
- **Classic rule 4.** One overall score through all rounds, reset only when a game ends.
- **Classic rule 5.** In Classic, a coin flip decides which bot sits where.
- **Classic rule 6.** In Classic, the target score is 100, 300, 500 or 1000. It is 300 unless the player picks another.

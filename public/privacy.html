STACKED — Privacy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

The short version. STACKED has no accounts and never asks for your name or email. We use Google Analytics to count plays and see how the game is used. You can turn that off anytime in Settings.

What's collected. When you play on stackedthegame.com, Google Analytics records:

  • pages viewed and basic page activity, like scrolling and link clicks
  • game moments: starting a game, your first capture, finishing a game (mode, win or loss, score, length, and Run level and stars), finishing the tutorial, completing The Run, and installing the app
  • the deal number of each game, so we can replay a deal that went wrong
  • your device and browser type, screen size, language, and rough location (city and country), which Google works out from your connection

Google sets a cookie in your browser with a random ID, so a returning visit counts as the same player.

Why. To see how many people play, where they get stuck, and what to fix next. We don't sell this data or use it for ads, and Google's ad-profile linking (Google signals) is off.

How long it's kept. Detailed activity is deleted after 2 months. Anything tied to your random ID is deleted 14 months after your last visit.

Turning it off. Open Settings (the gear on the home screen) and turn off "Share anonymous play data." It stays off on that browser and device. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, analytics stays off automatically. We don't respond to the older "Do Not Track" signal.

What stays on your device. Your saved games, Run progress, and tutorial and install markers live in your browser's storage. They're never sent to us. Clearing this site's data in your browser erases them.

Hosting. The site is served by Vercel, which handles your connection to deliver the pages, like any web host.

Kids. STACKED isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes. If this notice changes, we'll update this page and its date.
